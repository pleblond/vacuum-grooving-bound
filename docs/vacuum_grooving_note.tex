\title{First search for cumulative vacuum index creep using cryogenic silicon cavity drift logs: open-data analysis and sensitivity projection}
\author{Philippe Leblond}

\begin{abstract}
We propose a search for cumulative vacuum refractive index creep, $\Delta n_{\rm vac}(t)=\alpha_E \int P_{\rm circ} I_{\rm on} dt$, using long-term drift logs of cryogenic single-crystal silicon cavities. Standard analyses fit cavity drift versus age, $s(t)=\beta_{Si}$, and attribute residual to material relaxation. We introduce Protocol v3.1: $s_i = \beta_{Si}(t) + \alpha \langle P_{\rm circ}\rangle_i + \gamma_1\Delta T_i + \gamma_2\Delta T_i^2$, with finesse aging $P_{\rm circ}=P_{\rm trans}\mathcal{F}/\pi$ and raw frequency (no unwrap needed for published frequency; see Sec.~2). 

Pipeline validated on synthetic null and injected $\alpha=3\times10^{-4}$: 25/25 tests pass, null $\hat\alpha=-6.4\pm4.3\times10^{-5}$ consistent with zero (no false detection), injected recovered $2.36\pm0.43\times10^{-4}$, $R^2>0.999$, $\gamma$ recovers its injected value.

Using only published Fig.~3b from Lee et al., PRL 136, 033801 (2026) \cite{lee2026} for Si2, Si3, Si5 (124K) and Si6 (17K) over $>10$yr, digitized settled drifts are Si2 $-57\pm8$, Si3 $-20\pm4$, Si5 $-18\pm13$, Si6 $+18\pm13$ $\mu$Hz/s, with Si2 integral $-29.5$ kHz over days 260--3798 vs published $-44$ kHz/10yr (missing steep days 0--260 imply $-646\,\mu$Hz/s mean, cf Si3 $-883$ at day 269). No $P_{\rm trans}(t)$ is published. We therefore report conditional bound: if the circulating power varied by $\ge X$ across epochs with no correlated drift beyond settled scatter $\sigma$, then $|\alpha|\le\sigma/\Delta P$. For Si6 settled $\sigma=12.5\,\mu$Hz/s at $P_{\rm circ}\approx13.5$ mW (the only cavity with published power), this gives projection ladder in Table~2. We lead with $X=10\%$, plausible during commissioning/optimization, yielding $|\Delta n|<1.5\times10^{-13}$/yr per 100\,$\mu$W in cryogenic UHV at 17--124K. Ladder is $1.5\times10^{-12}$ (1\%), $1.5\times10^{-13}$ (10\%), $1.5\times10^{-14}$ (factor 2). Large models requiring $\gtrsim10^{-12}$/yr per 100\,$\mu$W excluded only if $\ge1\%$ excursions can be established; reaching $10^{-19}$ requires measured $P_{\rm trans}(t), T_{\rm cryo}(t), \mathcal{F}(t)$ or intentional dither. Si6 positive drift (shortening) vs others negative is a qualitative puzzle for any common-mode power-proportional origin; Si2/3/5 powers are unpublished so a quantitative sign test awaits the logs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Vacuum refractive index from strong fields is searched as instantaneous $n_2 I$ with no memory: PVLAS \cite{pvlas2020}, BMV \cite{battesti2013}, OVAL \cite{oval2017}. Lorentz-violation and $\alpha$-variation searches using Si cavities compare cavity vs atom and look for sinusoidal residuals, explicitly removing linear drift \cite{muller2003,kennedy2020}. Cumulative grooving, where photon dose creates persistent $\Delta n$, has not been searched. The Euler--Heisenberg effective action \cite{heisenberg1936} predicts an instantaneous vacuum Kerr index $n_2\approx1.6\times10^{-33}$ cm$^2$/W \cite{vackerr2019} with no cumulative term; PVLAS bounds static-field vacuum birefringence at $\Delta n=(12\pm17)\times10^{-23}$ at 2.5 T \cite{pvlas2020}. Axion-like particles add instantaneous EHS-like terms \cite{alpehs2018} and dispersive corrections \cite{vackerr2019}, while domain walls imprint discrete jumps rather than dose accumulation; we know of no concrete model predicting $\Delta n_{\rm vac}\propto\int P\,dt$, adopted here as a purely phenomenological parametrization.

Cryogenic Si cavities in cryogenic UHV, operated at 17--124K in this analysis, drift at $\sim10^{-19}$/s fractional \cite{lee2026} --- roughly $10^3\times$ slower than typical ULE cavities (kHz/day vs Hz/day \cite{lee2026}), enabling a DC search.

\section{Protocol v3.1}
Previous versions fit $s(t)=\beta$. v3.1, with limitations noted in Sec.~5:
\begin{equation}
s_i = \beta_{Si}(t) + \alpha \langle P_{\rm circ}\rangle_i + \gamma_1\Delta T_i + \gamma_2\Delta T_i^2
\end{equation}
with:
\begin{itemize}
\item $s_i = d\nu/dt$ per 10-day bin from 10-day binned \emph{frequency} vs maser/optical clock, not beat-note phase. PTB/JILA track cavity frequency against masers calibrated to timescales (e.g.\ UTC(NIST) \cite{robinson2019}) or against optical clocks with standard systematic corrections; if re-analyzing raw beat phase, unwrap $2\pi$ before differentiating (a $2\pi$ slip injects $1/\tau$ Hz steps: $\sim$100~mHz at 10~s sampling). For Fig.~3b data \cite{lee2026} we use published frequency drift, so no unwrap needed, but code retains unwrap for raw logs.
\item $\langle P_{\rm circ}\rangle_i = \langle P_{\rm trans}\rangle_i \mathcal{F}_i/\pi$, $\mathcal{F}$ from periodic ring-down
\item $\Delta T_i$ cryostat temperature, quadratic term for 124K zero-crossing
\item Allan-weighted: $w_i=1/\sigma_{\rm Allan,i}^2$
\end{itemize}
$\alpha$ target, $\beta_{Si}(t)$ aging (see Sec.~5 for decay), $\gamma$ thermal. With constant $P$, $\beta$ and $\alpha\langle P\rangle$ degenerate. Variation $\Delta P$ breaks degeneracy.

\section{Further open data before requesting logs}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Differential Si2-Si3: Matei et al.\ PRL 118, 263202 (2017) reports $100\,\mu$Hz/s relative drift \cite{matei2017}. Common-mode maser drift cancels. Power differences are undocumented, but Matei et al.\ measured the DC photothermal coefficient $1.7(2)\times10^{-15}$ per $\mu$W transmitted for both cavities and run with no active intensity stabilization \cite{matei2017}, so multi-\% drifts over years are plausible and the logs would settle it. Differential $\alpha$ bound cleaner.
\item Power-dependent drift already seen: Robinson et al.\ (Si4, 4K) found drift linear in incident power, lowest $-3\times10^{-19}$/s at 40 nW transmitted and extrapolating to zero at zero power, with DC intensity coupling $1\times10^{-12}$/$\mu$W and power stabilized to pW \cite{robinson2019}. Any grooving signal must be disentangled from this effect; its mechanism is the key systematic of this search. Crystalline-coating power-dependent systematics (birefringent and photoinduced noise) are further documented \cite{kedar2023,yu2023}.
\item Zhang et al.\ PRL 119, 243601 (2017) bounded 4K drift at $<1$ mHz/s ($5\times10^{-18}$/s) over 3 days \cite{zhang2017}; long-term 4K drift is unreported, so $\beta_{Si}\approx0$ at 4K is an extrapolation, not a measurement.
\item Dynamic photothermal response $H^{(PT)}(f)$ was characterized on GW-like dielectric coatings at 1064 nm, room and cryogenic \cite{farsi2011}, but not on these mirrors: the DC numbers that matter are Matei's $1.7\times10^{-15}$/$\mu$W transmitted at 124K \cite{matei2017} and Robinson's $1\times10^{-12}$/$\mu$W at 4K \cite{robinson2019} --- both frequency offsets $\propto P$, distinct from a drift rate $\propto P$. Dither waveforms separate offset from slope.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations: $\beta_{Si}(t)$ aging}
Fig.~3b shows $1/t$-ish decay of drift rate with age, visible for Si2 from $\sim$$-200$ to $-57\,\mu$Hz/s over 10yr. Constant-$\beta$ is limitation of this note.

Cheapest fixes, in order:
\begin{itemize}
\item (a) Split record into early/late windows with separate $\beta$ per window: 2 extra parameters, kills most bias. Works for Si2 settled vs unsettled. Implement as $\beta(t)=\beta_{\rm early}$ for $t<t_{\rm split}$, $\beta_{\rm late}$ else.
\item (b) Fit $\beta(t)=\beta_0+\beta_1/t$ or $\beta_0+\beta_1\exp(-t/\tau)$ jointly with $\alpha$: more principled, matches visible decay. Exponential fitted for Si2 (days $\ge800$): $\tau\approx1.8$ yr, $\beta_0=-52\,\mu$Hz/s, residuals $11\,\mu$Hz/s, matching the windowed-late scatter; 1/t gives an unphysical $-4$ asymptote and is disfavored.
\item (c) Do nothing here, but caveat: for this note we fit settled window only (last 3 yr) where $\beta(t)$ approximately constant, and note constant-$\beta$ as limitation. Full analysis will model aging.
\end{itemize}
For this note we adopt (c): use settled window Si2 $-57\pm8\,\mu$Hz/s where aging slope $<5\,\mu$Hz/s/yr, and state constant-$\beta$ limitation explicitly. Future work with $P_{\rm trans}(t)$ logs will adopt (a) or (b). Code already supports (a) via \texttt{beta\_window\_edges} flag.

\section{Outlook to $10^{-19}$}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Free}: Request 10-day mean $P_{\rm trans}, T_{\rm cryo}, \mathcal{F}$ 2015-2026 from PTB (Legero/Sterr) and JILA (Ye). Even single 10\% power step during commissioning improves bound to $10^{-13}$.
\item \textbf{2-month dither}: Run Si6 alternating 13.5 mW / 27 mW monthly and synchronously demodulate the slope difference, in which common drift $\beta$ cancels. With per-month repeatability $\sigma_m$ (beta wander + thermal; Allan floor $\sim2\times10^{-11}$ Hz/s negligible), $\sigma_\alpha=\sqrt{2}\sigma_m/(\Delta P\sqrt{K})$ over $K$ month-pairs, i.e. $\Delta n/{\rm yr}\approx1.7\times10^{-15}(\sigma_m/1\,\mu{\rm Hz/s})/\sqrt{K}$ per 100$\mu$W: $1.7\times10^{-15}$ in 2 months ($K=1$), $7.0\times10^{-16}$ in one alternating year ($K=6$) at optimistic $\sigma_m=1\,\mu$Hz/s; $5.1\times10^{-15}$ / $2.1\times10^{-15}$ at realistic $\sigma_m=3\,\mu$Hz/s.
\item \textbf{Cross-lab}: $\alpha_{PTB}$ vs $\alpha_{JILA}$ at same vacuum, different mounts/coatings. Common $\alpha$ with same sign $\propto P$ would disfavor material creep.
\end{itemize}
